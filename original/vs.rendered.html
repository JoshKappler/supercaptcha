Controlling AI agents

Pushary vs ClickUp, Trello, Zapier, Asana

Those tools manage human work. Pushary approves an autonomous agent's actions from your phone, with per-tool policy and an audit trail.

Pushary vs Happy

Happy is a free, open-source, end-to-end-encrypted mirror of Claude Code and Codex. Pushary adds policy, audit, and cross-vendor support.

Pushary vs Omnara

Omnara mirrors your full session through a daemon. Pushary minimizes readable decision data and encrypts synced transcripts on your machine for your phone and documented compliance recovery.

Pushary vs HumanLayer

HumanLayer today is an AI coding IDE for orchestrating agent sessions; its classic approvals SDK is deprecated. Pushary is live human-in-the-loop for the agents you run and ship.

Pushary vs Knock

Knock is notification infrastructure whose agent approval flow you assemble from webhooks. Pushary is the managed decision loop: one blocking call, fail-closed.

Pushary vs Courier

Courier sends across every channel and has no primitive that waits on a human. Pushary blocks until a person decides, and records it.

Pushary vs Novu

Novu posts approve/deny cards inside its chat-agent conversations. Pushary is a standalone decision loop any agent can call, with select and free-text answers.

Pushary vs Svix

Svix moves webhooks between machines. Pushary gets a decision from a person, on their phone, back to a waiting agent.

Pushary vs Conductor

Conductor runs parallel agents on your Mac. Pushary lets you approve and unblock them from your phone, on any OS.

Pushary vs Vibe Island

Vibe Island is a local-only Dynamic Island for your agents, and a good one. Pushary Isle is the same notch plus the phone, a policy engine and an audit trail, at a fifth of the memory.

Pushary vs Forge Remote

Forge Remote risk-scores each action in a native app. Pushary adds author-once policy, an audit trail, and any-MCP support.

Pushary vs Claude Code Notifier

Claude Code Notifier is a macOS notifier. Pushary adds human-in-the-loop, policy, and cross-platform support.

Pushary vs ntfy

ntfy is a free, open-source notification pipe. Pushary adds ask-and-wait approvals, per-tool policy, and an audit trail, and installs into the agents.

Pushary vs Pushover

Pushover is a simple, one-time-fee alert pipe. Pushary adds the agent approval loop, policy, and an exportable audit trail.

Pushary vs Pushcut

Pushcut builds actionable iOS notifications. Pushary is the cross-platform, agent-native approval layer with policy and audit.

Pushary vs Claude Code Remote Control

Anthropic's Remote Control is first-party and Claude-only. Pushary is the cross-vendor control plane with per-tool policy and an audit trail.

Pushary vs Claude Cowork Notifications

Cowork's built-in loop covers Cowork through the Claude app. Pushary is one inbox for every agent, with lock screen answers and an audit trail.
