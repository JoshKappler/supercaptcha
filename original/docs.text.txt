Push API (websites)
Pushary Documentation

Pushary sends your AI agent's questions and approvals to your phone. Set it up with one command, then approve, deny or answer from anywhere.

Copy Markdown
Open

Pushary is the decision layer for AI agents. When an agent like Claude Code, Codex or Cursor needs a yes before a risky step, Pushary sends the question to your phone or your Mac. You tap Approve or Deny and the agent keeps working. If you build agents, your users can approve your agent's actions the same way.

Copy Text
npx pushary@latest setup

Run it on the computer where your agent runs. It finds your agents, connects them, saves your API key and pairs your phone. The quickstart walks through each step.

Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Hermes and others ask you before risky steps. You answer on your phone or your Mac. One command sets it up.
