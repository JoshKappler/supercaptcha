Questions

Is my code sent to Pushary's servers?

Not on routine product paths. The decision path sends notification metadata: title, body text, tool name. Transcripts Pushary launches are redacted and encrypted on your machine.

What are permission hooks?

They catch Claude Code's tool approval prompts and send each one to your phone as a push notification.

Do I need the app?

No. Setup shows a QR code you scan once with your phone camera. Works on iOS and Android, in the app or the browser.
