Questions, answered.

How do I connect my phone?

Setup shows a QR code. Scan it once with your phone camera and questions start arriving on your lock screen.

Is my code sent to Pushary’s servers?

Not on routine product paths. The decision path sends notification metadata: title, body text and tool name. Transcripts that Pushary launches are redacted and encrypted on your machine, and your phone can decrypt them. Authorized Pushary compliance admins can recover them for documented legal obligations.

What are permission hooks?

Hooks catch Claude Code’s tool approval prompts and send them to you as push notifications, so the prompt no longer waits in a terminal you are not watching.

What can the agent ask me?

Three types of question: confirm (yes or no), select (2 to 6 options), and input (free text). Your agent picks the right one for the situation.
