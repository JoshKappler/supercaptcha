02The yes

Walk away. Come back to finished work.

  1. Your agent asks. It pauses at a step that needs your call: run a command, change a file, or pick between options.
  2. Your phone buzzes. Approve or deny from the notification, pick an option, or type a reply.
  3. Back to work. Your answer flows straight back to the agent. When the task finishes, you get a ping for that too.

Set the rules once. Safe steps you can undo run on their own. Deleting, deploying, spending money: those come to you. If you do not answer, your rule decides.
