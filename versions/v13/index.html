Questions, answered in the session.

The rest lives in the docs.

? Is my code sent to Pushary's servers?

Not on routine product paths. The decision path sends notification metadata: title, body text and tool name. Transcripts that Pushary launches are redacted and encrypted on your machine. Your phone can decrypt them, and authorized Pushary compliance admins can recover them for documented legal obligations.

? Where do the questions reach me?

Your phone, iOS or Android, in the app or the browser. Also your Mac's notch, Slack or the web app. Every answer is saved to an audit trail.

? What can an agent ask me?

A yes or no, a pick between 2 and 6 options, or a free text reply. The agent chooses the type.
